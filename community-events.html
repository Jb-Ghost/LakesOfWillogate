Event Guidelines & Rules

  • Events must be family-friendly and welcoming to all community members
  • No hate speech, discrimination, or unsafe activities
  • Events should promote community engagement and positive interaction
  • Alcohol and tobacco-related events require prior approval
  • Accurate date and time must be provided
  • Correct and verifiable location required
  • Clear event description (50-500 characters)
  • Valid organizer contact information
  • Events should be non-profit or community-oriented
  • All events are subject to approval by the Community Events Team
  • Approval typically takes 2-3 business days
  • We reserve the right to edit event details for clarity
  • Events may be removed if they violate these guidelines
  • Repeated violations may result in submission restrictions
  • Accepted: Community gatherings, workshops, classes, cleanups, sports events, arts festivals
  • Not Accepted: Commercial promotions, political campaigns, religious conversion events, multi-level marketing
  • Mixed-purpose events will be evaluated individually
