Volunteer Spotlight: Sarah's Summer Initiative

Sarah, a longtime community member, has launched a free art program for kids this summer at the recreation center. Every Tuesday and Thursday, she teaches drawing, painting, and sculpting. "Seeing the kids create something they're proud of is the best part of my week," Sarah shares. Join us in celebrating her dedication to our community!
