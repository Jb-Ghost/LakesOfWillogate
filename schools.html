Enrollment & Registration

Key information for enrolling your child

Katy ISD Enrollment

Online Registration

Complete enrollment online at katyisd.org before visiting campus. All registration must be completed through the district portal.

Attendance Boundaries

Use the district boundary tool at katyisd.org to determine which schools serve your address.

Key Programs

  • Pre-K Programs
  • Magnet Programs (STEM, Fine Arts)
  • Gifted & Talented
  • Career & Technical Education
  • Transfer & Open Enrollment

Lamar CISD Enrollment

Online Registration

Complete enrollment online at lcisd.org before visiting campus. All registration must be completed through the district portal.

Attendance Boundaries

Use the zone finder at lcisd.org to determine which schools serve your address.

Key Programs

  • Pre-K Programs
  • Dual Language Programs
  • Gifted & Talented
  • Career & Technical Education
  • Transfer Options

Required Documents for Enrollment

Have these documents ready when registering your child:

Proof of Residency

Current lease/mortgage, utility bill, or affidavit of residence

Birth Certificate

Official birth certificate (certified copy)

Immunization Records

Up-to-date Texas immunization records

Photo ID

Parent/guardian driver's license or state ID

Social Security Card

Student's social security card (if available)

Previous School Records

Report cards, transcripts (if transferring)
