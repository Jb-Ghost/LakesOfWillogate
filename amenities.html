Pool Rules & Safety

  • Swimwear required at all times
  • Children under 12 must be supervised by an adult
  • No running on pool deck
  • No glass containers or outside food
  • Shower before entering pool
  • Follow lifeguard instructions at all times
  • No diving in shallow areas
  • No alcohol or smoking permitted

Park Rules & Guidelines

  • Parks close at sunset unless posted otherwise
  • No littering – use designated trash bins
  • Pets must be leashed and under control
  • No alcohol or smoking in parks
  • Respect wildlife and native plants
  • Keep noise levels reasonable
  • Stay on marked trails and paths
  • No unauthorized vehicles on park grounds
