Community Guidelines

General Community Rules

  • Treat all community members, staff, and facilities with respect
  • Follow posted hours of operation for all facilities
  • Children under 14 must be accompanied by an adult
  • Proper attire required at all facilities
  • No smoking, alcohol, or illegal substances on community property

Pool & Aquatic Center Guidelines

  • Swim at your own risk - lifeguards on duty during posted hours
  • Shower before entering the pool
  • No running, diving in shallow areas, or rough play
  • Appropriate swimwear required
  • Glass containers prohibited in pool areas

Fitness Center Guidelines

  • Wipe down equipment after each use
  • Return weights and equipment to proper storage
  • Wear athletic shoes and appropriate workout attire
  • Use headphones for personal audio devices
  • Time limits may apply during peak hours

Parks & Recreation Guidelines

  • Keep parks clean - dispose of trash properly
  • Pets must be leashed and waste must be picked up
  • Respect designated areas for sports and activities
  • No motorized vehicles on trails or lawns
  • Park closes at dusk unless otherwise posted
